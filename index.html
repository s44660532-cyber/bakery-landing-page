Our Story

One oven, two bakers, and a very early alarm.

Golden Crumb started in a rented kitchen with a single deck oven and a starter named "Dolores" that's still alive today. Three decades later we're a crew of fourteen, but the method hasn't changed: real flour, long fermentation, no shortcuts.

We mill grain from farms within 60 miles, bake in small batches, and donate whatever's left at closing to the neighborhood shelter.

  • Stone-milled local grain
  • No preservatives, ever
  • Daily donations to the community fridge
